Who Regulates You

More than one agency has a say

There is no single Texas licence that covers everything a tattoo studio does. These are the bodies whose rules your documentation has to satisfy.

DSHS

Studio licensing and operating standards under Health & Safety Code Chapter 146 and 25 TAC Chapter 229.

TCEQ

Medical waste handling and transport under 30 TAC Chapter 326.

Federal OSHA

Bloodborne pathogens rules apply once you have employees; universal precautions apply to everyone.

Why It Matters

What's actually at stake

Real, published maximums — not scare numbers. Every figure below is a statutory or regulatory cap, not a typical assessment.

Each day can count separately

DSHS may assess administrative penalties of up to $5,000, and under 25 TAC §229.412 each day of a continuing violation can be treated as a separate violation. Operating unlicensed is treated the same way.

Records are the inspection

Sterilization logs, monthly spore-test results, and client files are what an inspector asks for first — and they have to be retained (at least two years for sterilization logs and client records).

OSHA applies once you have staff

Federal OSHA maximums, effective January 15, 2026: $16,550 per serious violation and $165,514 per willful or repeated violation. Texas has no state OSHA plan for private employers, so these apply directly.

Figures are maximum penalties published by the agencies themselves and verified as of August 2026. Actual assessments vary widely with the facts. Read the full Texas Tattoo Studio compliance guide for the underlying rules.

FAQ

Questions tattoo studio owners ask

How often do sterilizers need spore testing?

Every calendar month, through an approved laboratory, with the results retained for inspection (25 TAC §229.407). The Kit includes the monthly log.

What has to be in a client record?

The service and body location, ink colors with manufacturer and lot detail, the artist, aftercare acknowledgment, and the client's signature — kept at least two years. The Kit's consent form captures all of it in one page.

Can I tattoo someone under 18?

Texas allows tattooing a minor only in a narrow cover-up circumstance with parental consent; piercings require written, notarized parental consent with presence or affidavit. The Kit includes both forms so the file is complete.

I'm a solo artist with no employees. Does OSHA apply?

The federal bloodborne pathogens standard is an employer obligation, so a true solo operator isn't covered as an employer — but DSHS rules still require universal precautions and sanitation. The Kit covers the DSHS side either way and includes the OSHA set for when you hire.

Do I need a separate license for piercing?

Your DSHS license has to cover the services you actually offer — tattoo, piercing, or both. The license tracker makes the match explicit.
