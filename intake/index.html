The business

Your documents carry your legal name and address, so these need to match what is on your licenses and filings.

People and roles

Several documents have to name a specific person — a privacy officer, a safety coordinator, a delegating physician. If one person wears several hats, just repeat the name.

What you actually do

This decides which protocols, consents, and logs belong in your Kit. Tick everything you offer — leaving one out means a missing document, and ticking one you do not do just adds paperwork you will not use.

Vendors and disposal

Vendors that touch patient information need a signed agreement, and we prepare those with the vendor named. If you do not use one, write “none”.

Be honest here — it changes nothing about the price and it means we can flag what to do first. “Nothing” is a perfectly normal answer.
