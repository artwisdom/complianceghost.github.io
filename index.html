Compliance Shield tracks the changes and updates the manual you bought. Choose monthly ($89/mo) or annual ($890/year — two months free). Your first 30 days are free when you add it to your Kit. Shield keeps your Kit current, so it's sold with the Kit or added by existing Kit owners.

How much could a violation actually cost me?

Federal OSHA maximums are $16,550 per serious violation and $165,514 per willful or repeated violation (effective January 15, 2026). Texas privacy penalties under Health & Safety Code §181.201 run to $250,000 where protected information is used for financial gain, and up to $1.5 million a year for a pattern or practice. The $997 Kit costs less than one serious OSHA citation. These are published maximums, not typical assessments.
